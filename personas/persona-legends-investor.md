### PART B-0: BEFORE NORTHSTAR — A Day in Catherine's Life

Catherine arrives at her sunroom desk at 8:15am. The room smells of the Diptyque candle she lit ten minutes ago. She opens her iMac and the first tab is always **Addepar**. 

**The Addepar Grind (9:00am - 11:30am)**:
It’s the second week of the quarter. This is the "Data Entry Ritual." Catherine has investments with 6 different GPs. Three of them use **Juniper Square**, which she loves. She logs into the Juniper Square portal for Fund A, clicks "Documents," and sees the Q4 report, the capital activity statement, and the tax K-1 all neatly organized. The data in Juniper Square is real-time; she can see her IRR and MOIC without lifting a finger. She takes a screenshot of the performance dashboard and hands it to her assistant, Margaret, to update the "Shadow Sheet."

Then comes the pain. The other three GPs—including Tri-Star (pre-NorthStar)—send PDFs via email or use clunky, "homegrown" portals. Catherine has to:
1. Search her inbox for "Tri-Star Q4 Report."
2. Download the 42-page PDF.
3. Manually extract the "Invested Capital," "Distributions," and "Current NAV" figures.
4. Open her master Excel "Shadow Model."
5. Type the numbers in.
6. Cross-reference the "Distributions" against her bank statement to ensure the wire actually hit.
7. Flag a $12.50 discrepancy in a wire fee that David Park (Finance) missed.

**The Juniper Square Benchmark**:
Catherine compares every interaction to Juniper Square. "If a $50M fund can afford Juniper Square, why is a $200M fund sending me password-protected PDFs?" she mutters. To her, a professional portal isn't a luxury; it's a signal of institutional maturity. When a GP doesn't have one, she assumes their back office is "two guys and a spreadsheet," which triggers her "Miami Hotel Failure" PTSD.

**The K-1 Waiting Game**:
It’s March 12th. She is still missing two K-1s. Her tax advisor at PwC is breathing down her neck. She has filed an extension every year for the last five years. "The K-1 is the ultimate test of a GP's competence," she tells Margaret. "If they can't get me a tax document by March 15th, how can I trust them to manage a $50M construction budget?"

**Handoff Friction**:
Catherine waits on Margaret to reconcile the Addepar data. Margaret waits on the GPs to send the reports. The GPs wait on their third-party fund admins. The chain is fragile. Last year, a GP sent an "Amended K-1" in August, forcing Catherine to amend her entire family office tax return. The accounting fees alone were $8,500.

**End-of-Day State**:
By 5:00pm, the "Shadow Model" is 80% updated. She still has three "Magic Links" to portals she hasn't logged into because she can't remember which email address she used for the registration. She closes the iMac, feeling the familiar "data fatigue."

She spent 4 minutes in NorthStar. She found 3 data errors. She won't log in again for 90 days.
