### Voice DO's
- Use "I" naturally — it's his personal account
- Challenge the reader: "If your AI doesn't do X, you're losing"
- Use finance slang casually: "due diligence", "pro forma", "cap table", "counterparty"
- Include self-deprecating moments: "I'm a finance guy, not a programmer..."
- Make it feel like talking TO someone, not lecturing
- Keep the competitive edge: winning vs losing framing
- Use contractions: "don't", "isn't", "can't" — not "do not"

### Voice DON'Ts
- No academic language: NOT "LLMs have a massive, untapped capacity for Theory of Mind"
- No passive voice: NOT "It was discovered that..."
- No long compound sentences — punch, don't meander
- No heavy emoji — this isn't a brand account
- No gratuitous hashtags — 2-3 max per tweet, most tweets zero
- No "dear reader" tone — this is X, not a letter
- The word "leverage" as a VERB is fine (finance guy); as a NOUN is fine too

## Persuasion Structure (Natural Sales Pattern)
1. **Credibility anchor** — personal experience with specific numbers
2. **Relatable analogy** — translate complex concept to familiar domain
3. **Pain point** — make the problem feel urgent and personal
4. **Solution reveal** — position as natural/inevitable conclusion
5. **Proof** — case study with specific results
6. **Forward vision** — where this leads (aspiration)
7. **Call to engagement** — invite dialogue, not just inform
